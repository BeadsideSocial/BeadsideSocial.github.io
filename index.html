How a creative social works

  1. Pick your date

    Book a party at your place or an event for your team. Once our studio reopens, you can also reserve a small private party or a seat at an open creative social.

  2. Settle in

    Grab a seat and a drink, catch up, and browse a curated spread of supplies for your craft, sorted and ready to go.

  3. Get your creative fix

    Beading, candles, totes, tumblers, or something new: we walk you through your chosen craft step by step. No experience needed, and help is always an arm's length away.

  4. Take it home

    Two to three hours later, everyone leaves with a finished creation and a few photos worth posting.

A sample 2½-hour party

  1. 0:00–0:15

    What's on the bead bar

    Beading is where we started, and it's still one of our favorite crafts. For beading parties, everything you need is waiting at the table, from findings and closures to the beads that make each piece yours.

    Always something newWe're constantly sourcing new beads, charms, and findings, so the bead bar keeps changing with fresh colors and styles. Come back for your next party and you'll find something you haven't seen before.

    01

    Ear wires & closures

    The part that goes in the ear, and the foundation of every pair.

    • French hooks (fishhooks)Curved hooks that slip easily into pierced ears, often paired with a silicone stopper.
    • LeverbacksHinged ear wires that snap shut in the back, extra secure for heavier dangles.
    • Earring posts (studs)Straight pins with a flat pad or loop in front, for a clean, close-to-the-ear look.
    • HoopsWire hoops in endless, hinged, or snap styles, ready to bead or dangle from.
    • Earring backsButterfly clutch backs and silicone stoppers to keep everything in place.

    02

    Jewelry findings

    The connectors and components that hold your design together.

    • HeadpinsWires with a flat or ball "head" that keeps beads from sliding off, so you can form a loop at the top.
    • Eye pinsWires with a pre-formed loop at one end for linking bead sections together.
    • Open jump ringsSmall metal rings for attaching dangles, charms, and beads to your ear wires.
    • Extender chainsLengths of chain for adding movement, drop length, and layered dangles.

    03

    Beads & charms

    The decorative heart of your design: color, texture, and personality.

    • Natural semi-precious stones & gemsEarthy, one-of-a-kind stones in a range of colors.
    • Glass beadsBright and shimmering, in endless shapes and finishes.
    • Acrylic beadsLightweight and playful, great for bold statement pieces.
    • Seed & Delica beadsTiny, uniform beads for fringe, patterns, and fine detail.
    • Pearls & shellsClassic coastal elegance with a natural glow.
    • Polymer clay & ceramicHandmade-look beads with fun shapes and matte, earthy textures.
    • Charms & dropsSmall pendants and drops to finish off a dangle.

    04

    Spacer beads

    Little accents that add polish and balance between your main beads.

    • Round ball spacersSimple metal beads that add a touch of shine.
    • RondellesFlattened, doughnut-shaped beads that frame larger stones nicely.
    • Daisy spacersDelicate flower-shaped accents with a dainty feel.
    • Textured discsPatterned discs that add sparkle and dimension.

    Meet the makers

    We're Monica and David, a sister and brother who grew up in a big, creative family. Someone was always painting, shaping pottery, or making something new, and they made sure we had room to try it all. That's where our love for the arts began.

    Over the years, those skills became how we entertained our kids and family at parties, how we spent our free time, and how we unwound from busy, high-stress jobs. And we kept seeing the same thing: a table full of supplies brings people together. Everyone has fun, breaks out of the routine, and goes home with something they made.

    We're crafty at home, too. We love reusing, repurposing, and giving old things a new life, and our own houses are proof that every update can be a fun project.

    Beadside Social is our way of sharing all of that with you: small, private parties in our Palm Beach County studio, where we can sit with every guest and help with the tricky parts, and on-location parties and events of any size, where we bring the whole party to you. Either way, everyone leaves with a piece they love.

    From our family to yours, thanks for being here. We can't wait to see what you make.

    Questions

    Do I need any crafting experience?

    None at all. Most guests are first-timers. We teach a few simple techniques for your craft and help as you go.

    Where are parties held?

    Right now, at your place. While our studio gets a glow-up, we're booking on-location parties and corporate events: we bring the party to your home, office, or venue, along with all the supplies, and tables, chairs, and linens if you need them. Once the studio reopens, we'll host small private parties there again. Because it's reservation-only, we share the studio address once your date is confirmed.

    Do you travel outside Palm Beach County?

    Yes! We're based in Palm Beach County, and we're happy to bring the party north or south of us, to neighboring counties like Martin and St. Lucie or Broward and Miami-Dade. Depending on your number of guests and the package you choose, we're willing to travel, and a travel fee may apply. Just add your location and headcount to your request, and we'll let you know what we can do.

    Can I come by myself?

    Absolutely. Open creative socials are made for that, and they'll return when the studio reopens. Book a single seat (or two) and join a small group at the craft table.

    How many people can come?

    It depends on where we meet. In our studio, parties stay small and private, with 6 to 8 guests, so everyone gets attention. On-location parties and corporate events have no participant limit, so invite the whole family or the whole team.

    Is it only beads and jewelry?

    Not at all. Beading is where we started, and with beads we make jewelry, ornaments, and home decor. Beyond beads, we're set up for other crafts like canvas tote decorating, candle making, and tumbler decorating. Have something else in mind? Ask us. The sky's the limit.

    Can we bring wine?

    Parties at our studio are alcohol-free, and every party includes complimentary non-alcoholic drinks. If we come to you for a Traveling Social, you're welcome to serve your own drinks.

    Can we bring our own food or cake?

    Yes! If you're celebrating a birthday, shower, or other occasion, you're welcome to bring your own cake and food. You can also add sweet and savory snacks to your package, or ask us about our catering and bakery partners for something more elaborate or themed.

    Is there a minimum age?

    Guests 8 and up are welcome. Kids under 12 need an adult present, and our Kids & Teen Social is designed just for younger makers.

    What days and times do you host?

    Any time that works for you. We host brunch crafting, afternoon parties, after-work team events, and evening get-togethers, on weekdays and weekends.

    How far ahead should I book?

    On-location dates are open now. We host mornings, afternoons, and evenings, any day of the week. Weekends go first, so a few weeks ahead is a good idea, especially for showers, birthdays, and company events with a fixed date. Larger groups need a bit more lead time for supplies.
