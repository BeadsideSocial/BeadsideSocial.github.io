How a night works

  1. Pick your date

    Send a request with your occasion and group size. Every party is private and by reservation only, so the table is just yours.

  2. Settle in

    Grab a seat and a drink, catch up, and browse a curated spread of glass, stone, and metal beads sorted by color.

  3. Make your pair

    We walk you through beaded and wire-wrapped earrings step by step. No experience needed, and help is always an arm's length away.

  4. Wear them home

    Two to three hours later, everyone leaves with finished jewelry and a few photos worth posting.

A sample 2½-hour party

  1. 0:00–0:15
    Welcome

    Arrive, grab a drink and a snack, and take a few photos at the backdrop.

  2. 0:15–0:30
    Quick demo

    Learn the three moves behind every design: a simple loop, opening a jump ring, and attaching a hook.

  3. 0:30–1:45
    Make your first pair

    Design and build at your own pace, with one-on-one help whenever you want it.

  4. 1:45–2:10
    Second pair and finishing touches

    Start another pair or add the final details to your first.

  5. 2:10–2:25
    Show and tell

    Group photos, then we card and package your earrings to take home.

  6. 2:25–2:30
    Wrap-up

    Goodbyes, plus a discount code for your next party.

Every seat has a starter design card, so even first-timers leave with a finished, wearable pair.

Questions

Do I need any jewelry-making experience?

None at all. Most guests have never held a pair of pliers. We teach a few simple techniques and help as you go.

Where are parties held?

In our private studio space in Palm Beach County. Because it's reservation-only, we share the address once your date is confirmed. With The Private Social, we can also bring the party to your home.

How many people can come?

Most parties are 6 to 8 guests, small enough that everyone gets attention.

Is it only earrings?

Earrings are where we start, since you can finish a pair in one sitting. More pieces are on the way.

Can we bring wine?

Parties at our studio are alcohol-free, and we serve mocktails and sparkling drinks. If we come to your home for a Private Social, you're welcome to serve your own drinks.

Is there a minimum age?

Guests 12 and up are welcome at studio parties.

How far ahead should I book?

Weekend evenings go first, so a few weeks ahead is a good idea, especially for showers and birthdays with a fixed date.
