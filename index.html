How a night works

  1. Pick your date

    Book a private party for your group, or reserve a single seat at an open social night. Everything is by reservation only.

  2. Settle in

    Grab a seat and a drink, catch up, and browse a curated spread of glass, stone, and metal beads sorted by color.

  3. Make your pair

    We walk you through beaded and wire-wrapped earrings step by step. No experience needed, and help is always an arm's length away.

  4. Wear them home

    Two to three hours later, everyone leaves with finished jewelry and a few photos worth posting.

A sample 2½-hour party

  1. 0:00–0:15
    Welcome

    Arrive, grab a drink and a snack, and take a few photos at the backdrop.

  2. 0:15–0:30
    Quick demo

    Learn the three moves behind every design: a simple loop, opening a jump ring, and attaching a hook.

  3. 0:30–1:45
    Make your first pair

    Design and build at your own pace, with one-on-one help whenever you want it.

  4. 1:45–2:10
    Second pair and finishing touches

    Start another pair or add the final details to your first.

  5. 2:10–2:25
    Show and tell

    Group photos, then we card and package your earrings to take home.

  6. 2:25–2:30
    Wrap-up

    Goodbyes, plus a discount code for your next party.

Every seat has a starter design card, so even first-timers leave with a finished, wearable pair.

    What's on the bead bar

    Everything you need to design your own earrings is waiting at the table, from the wires that go in your ear to the beads that make them yours.

    Always something newWe're constantly sourcing new beads, charms, and findings, so the bead bar keeps changing with fresh colors and styles. Come back for your next party and you'll find something you haven't seen before.

    01

    Ear wires & closures

    The part that goes in the ear, and the foundation of every pair.

    • French hooks (fishhooks)Curved hooks that slip easily into pierced ears, often paired with a silicone stopper.
    • LeverbacksHinged ear wires that snap shut in the back, extra secure for heavier dangles.
    • Earring posts (studs)Straight pins with a flat pad or loop in front, for a clean, close-to-the-ear look.
    • HoopsWire hoops in endless, hinged, or snap styles, ready to bead or dangle from.
    • Earring backsButterfly clutch backs and silicone stoppers to keep everything in place.

    02

    Jewelry findings

    The connectors and components that hold your design together.

    • HeadpinsWires with a flat or ball "head" that keeps beads from sliding off, so you can form a loop at the top.
    • Eye pinsWires with a pre-formed loop at one end for linking bead sections together.
    • Open jump ringsSmall metal rings for attaching dangles, charms, and beads to your ear wires.
    • Extender chainsLengths of chain for adding movement, drop length, and layered dangles.

    03

    Beads & charms

    The decorative heart of your design: color, texture, and personality.

    • Natural semi-precious stones & gemsEarthy, one-of-a-kind stones in a range of colors.
    • Glass beadsBright and shimmering, in endless shapes and finishes.
    • Acrylic beadsLightweight and playful, great for bold statement pieces.
    • Seed & Delica beadsTiny, uniform beads for fringe, patterns, and fine detail.
    • Pearls & shellsClassic coastal elegance with a natural glow.
    • Polymer clay & ceramicHandmade-look beads with fun shapes and matte, earthy textures.
    • Charms & dropsSmall pendants and drops to finish off a dangle.

    04

    Spacer beads

    Little accents that add polish and balance between your main beads.

    • Round ball spacersSimple metal beads that add a touch of shine.
    • RondellesFlattened, doughnut-shaped beads that frame larger stones nicely.
    • Daisy spacersDelicate flower-shaped accents with a dainty feel.
    • Textured discsPatterned discs that add sparkle and dimension.

    Meet the makers

And we kept seeing the same thing: a table full of supplies brings people together. Everyone has fun, breaks out of the routine, and goes home with something they made.

    Questions

    Do I need any jewelry-making experience?

    None at all. Most guests have never held a pair of pliers. We teach a few simple techniques and help as you go.

    Where are parties held?

    In our private studio space in Palm Beach County. Because it's reservation-only, we share the address once your date is confirmed. With The Private Social, we can also bring the party to your home.

    Can I come by myself?

    Absolutely. Open social nights are made for that. Book a single seat (or two) and join a small group at the bead bar.

    How many people can come?

    Most parties are 6 to 8 guests, small enough that everyone gets attention.

    Is it only earrings?

    Earrings are where we start, since you can finish a pair in one sitting. More pieces are on the way.

    Can we bring wine?

    Parties at our studio are alcohol-free, and we serve mocktails and sparkling drinks. If we come to your home for a Private Social, you're welcome to serve your own drinks.

    Is there a minimum age?

    Guests 8 and up are welcome. Kids under 12 need an adult present, and our Kids & Teen Social is designed just for younger makers.

    How far ahead should I book?

    Weekend evenings go first, so a few weeks ahead is a good idea, especially for showers and birthdays with a fixed date.
